Welcome to the Full-Stack Engineer Path, a 19-week training path.

## How to use this

- Work through the weeks in order. Each one ends with a **Done when** checklist.
- Tick items as you finish them. Your progress is saved in this browser only, and the timeline below marks a week complete once every item is ticked.
- Tracks are reference pages. Each week lists the tracks to read alongside it.
