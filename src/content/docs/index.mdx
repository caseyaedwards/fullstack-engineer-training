The Full-Stack Engineer Path is a 19-week training path from QA and frontend work to full-stack engineering, with backend and operations as the main focus.

## How to Use This Site

- Work through the weeks in order. Each week ends with a **Done When** checklist.
- Tick items as you finish them. Your progress stays in this browser: other browsers and devices do not see it. The timeline below marks a week complete when you tick every item of that week.
- Tracks are reference pages. Each week lists the tracks to read alongside it.
